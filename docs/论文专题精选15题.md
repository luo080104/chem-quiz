# 论文专题精选 15 题（Following the Arrow）

### 1. [1a-52] According to the Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle, it is impossible to know precisely both the position and the ________ of an electron.

- A) mass
- B) color
- C) momentum
- D) shape
- E) charge

**答案：C**

**解析**：不确定性原理：无法同时精确知道电子的位置与动量（momentum）。

**翻译**：根据海森堡不确定性原理，不可能同时精确知道电子的位置和它的 ________。

**难词**：Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle=海森堡不确定性原理；precisely=精确地；position=位置；momentum=动量

### 2. [1a-54] The photoelectric effect is ________.

- A) the total reflection of light by metals giving them their typical luster
- B) the production of current by silicon solar cells when exposed to sunlight
- C) the ejection of electrons by a metal when struck with light of sufficient energy
- D) the darkening of photographic film when exposed to an electric field
- E) a relativistic effect

**答案：C**

**解析**：光电效应：金属被足够能量（频率）的光照射时逸出电子。

**翻译**：光电效应是 ________。

**难词**：photoelectric effect=光电效应；ejection=逸出、发射；luster=光泽；relativistic=相对论的

### 3. [1a-56] The uncertainty principle states that ________.

- A) matter and energy are really the same thing
- B) it is impossible to know anything with certainty
- C) it is impossible to know the exact position and momentum of an electron
- D) there can only be one uncertain digit in a reported number
- E) it is impossible to know how many electrons there are in an atom

**答案：C**

**解析**：不确定性原理指无法同时精确确定电子的位置和动量。

**翻译**：不确定性原理指出 ________。

**难词**：uncertainty principle=不确定性原理；with certainty=确定地；exact=精确的

### 4. [1a-63] Which electron configuration represents a violation of the Pauli exclusion principle?

- A) [图片选项]
- B) [图片选项]
- C) [图片选项]
- D) [图片选项]
- E) [图片选项]

### 5. [1a-64] The ________ quantum number defines the shape of an orbital.

- A) spin

### 7. [1a-68] How many quantum numbers are necessary to designate a particular electron in an atom?

### 8. [1b-5] The ability of an atom in a molecule to attract electrons is best quantified by the ________.

- A) paramagnetism
- B) diamagnetism
- C) electronegativity
- D) electron charge-to-mass ratio
- E) first ionization potential

**答案：C**

**解析**：电负性（electronegativity）衡量分子中原子吸引电子的能力。

**翻译**：分子中原子吸引电子的能力最好用 ________ 来量化。

**难词**：attract=吸引；quantified=量化；electronegativity=电负性；paramagnetism=顺磁性；diamagnetism=抗磁性

- C) [图片选项]
- D) [图片选项]

### 12. [1b-36] A typical double bond ________.

- A) is stronger and shorter than a single bond
- B) consists of one σ bond and one π bond
- C) imparts rigidity to a molecule
- D) consists of two shared electron pairs
- E) All of the above answers are correct.

**答案：E**

**解析**：双键比单键更强更短、含1个σ+1个π、由2对电子构成并使分子刚性，全对。

**翻译**：典型的双键 ________。

**难词**：sigma bond=σ 键；pi bond=π 键；rigidity=刚性

### 13. [1b-37] Electrons in ________ bonds remain localized between two atoms. Electrons in ________ bonds can become delocalized between more than two atoms.

- A) pi, sigma
- B) sigma, pi
- C) pi, pi
- D) sigma, sigma
- E) ionic, sigma

**答案：B**

**解析**：σ键电子定域在两原子间；π键电子可离域到两个以上原子。

**翻译**：________ 键中的电子定域在两个原子之间；________ 键中的电子可离域到两个以上原子之间。

**难词**：localized=定域的；delocalized=离域的；sigma=σ（西格玛）；pi=π（派）

### 14. [1b-38] The bond order of any molecule containing equal numbers of bonding and antibonding electrons is ________.

- A) 0
- B) 1
- C) 2
- D) 3
- E) 1/2

**答案：A**

**解析**：键级=(成键电子−反键电子)/2，两者相等时键级为0。

**翻译**：任何成键电子数与反键电子数相等的分子，其键级是 ________。

**难词**：bond order=键级；bonding electrons=成键电子；antibonding electrons=反键电子

### 15. [1b-40] Based on molecular orbital theory, there is/are ________ unpaired electron(s) in the OF+ ion.

- A) 0
- B) 3
- C) 1
- D) 2
- E) 1/2

**答案：D**

**解析**：OF⁺ 有12个价电子，与 O₂ 等电子，π* 轨道上有2个未成对电子。【独立复算，待核】

**翻译**：根据分子轨道理论，OF⁺ 离子中有 ________ 个未成对电子。

**难词**：molecular orbital theory=分子轨道理论；unpaired electrons=未成对电子
