# 论文相关题目清单：论文专题：电子·轨道·化学键（Following the Arrow）

配合随笔《Following the Arrow》。从 200 题中抽取 34 题，可在 App 首页“论文专题”里单独顺序练习（路径 #/topic/arrow）。

> 论文讨论“电子在反应中究竟如何移动”：弯箭头的含义、电子密度与概率、轨道形状、H₂ 成键、部分电荷 δ、共振、以及超快光谱/电子衍射的测量。下列题目覆盖这些概念。注：题库为入门大学化学，不含“弯箭头机理”题；最接近的是 Lewis/形式电荷/共振/分子轨道等电子记账题。

## 量子、波与测量

- **1a-22** Of the following, the smallest and lightest subatomic particle is the ________.
  - 关联：电子是最小最轻的亚原子粒子（论文讨论“移动的是电子”）。
- **1a-51** Of the following, ________ radiation has the shortest wavelength.
  - 关联：电磁波谱：X 射线波长最短。
- **1a-52** According to the Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle, it is impossible to know precisely both the position and the ________ of an electron.
  - 关联：海森堡不确定性原理：位置与动量不能同时精确。
- **1a-54** The photoelectric effect is ________.
  - 关联：光电效应：足够能量的光把电子从金属打出。
- **1a-56** The uncertainty principle states that ________.
  - 关联：不确定性原理的表述。
- **1a-70** What wavelengths correspond to the visible region of the electromagnetic spectrum?
  - 关联：可见光波长约 400–750 nm。

## 原子轨道与电子排布

- **1a-55** All of the orbitals in a given electron shell have the same value as the ________ quantum number.
  - 关联：同一电子层轨道的主量子数相同。
- **1a-57** Which one of the following orbitals can hold two electrons?
  - 关联：一个轨道最多容纳 2 个电子。
- **1a-62** The lowest orbital energy is reached when the number of electrons with the same spin is maximized. This statement describes ________.
  - 关联：洪特规则：同亚层电子尽量分占轨道且自旋平行。
- **1a-63** Which electron configuration represents a violation of the Pauli exclusion principle?
  - 关联：泡利不相容（图片题）：同轨道两电子自旋必须相反——正是论文强调的“同一轨道成对”。
- **1a-64** The ________ quantum number defines the shape of an orbital.
  - 关联：角动量量子数决定轨道形状。
- **1a-65** The n = 1 shell contains ________ p orbitals. All the other shells contain ________ p orbitals.
  - 关联：n=1 层没有 p 轨道。
- **1a-66** There are ________ orbitals in the second shell.
  - 关联：第二层共 4 个轨道。
- **1a-67** ________-orbitals are spherically symmetrical.
  - 关联：s 轨道球形对称（论文原句：1s 球形，p 双叶带节面）。
- **1a-68** How many quantum numbers are necessary to designate a particular electron in an atom?
  - 关联：描述一个电子需 4 个量子数。

## 化学键与分子轨道

- **1b-6** In the nitrite ion (NO2), ________.
  - 关联：亚硝酸根两键因共振完全等同。
- **1b-8** The Lewis structure of the CO32- ion is ________.
  - 关联：碳酸根 CO₃²⁻ 的 Lewis 结构（共振）。
- **1b-20** The formal charge on carbon in the molecule below is ________. 
  - 关联：CO₂ 中碳的形式电荷（电子记账）。
- **1b-21** There are ________ unpaired electrons in the Lewis symbol for an oxygen atom.
  - 关联：氧原子 Lewis 符号中的未成对电子。
- **1b-36** A typical double bond ________.
  - 关联：双键由 1 个 σ + 1 个 π 组成。
- **1b-37** Electrons in ________ bonds remain localized between two atoms. Electrons in ________ bonds can become delocalized between more than two atoms.
  - 关联：σ 电子定域、π 电子可离域（对应论文“电子密度可变化/离域”）。
- **1b-38** The bond order of any molecule containing equal numbers of bonding and antibonding electrons is ________.
  - 关联：成键与反键电子数相等时键级为 0。
- **1b-39** In comparing the same two atoms bonded together, the ________ the bond order, the ________ the bond length, and the ________ the bond energy.
  - 关联：键级越大→键长越短、键能越大。
- **1b-40** Based on molecular orbital theory, there is/are ________ unpaired electron(s) in the OF+ ion.
  - 关联：OF⁺ 的分子轨道未成对电子（与 O₂ 等电子）。

## 部分电荷、电负性与极性

- **1a-73** The effective nuclear charge of an atom is primarily affected by ________.
  - 关联：有效核电荷（核电荷 vs 内层电子屏蔽）——与“电子被拉向谁”有关。
- **1a-80** Of the following elements, ________ has the most negative electron affinity.
  - 关联：电子亲和能最负的元素。
- **1b-5** The ability of an atom in a molecule to attract electrons is best quantified by the ________.
  - 关联：电负性：分子中原子吸引电子的能力（论文 δ−/δ+ 的基础）。
- **1b-19** Electronegativity ________ from left to right within a period and ________ from top to bottom within a group.
  - 关联：电负性在周期/族中的趋势。
- **1b-24** Of the atoms below, ________ is the most electronegative.
  - 关联：电负性最大的元素。
- **1b-30** The molecular geometry of the PF3 molecule is ________, and this molecule is ________.
  - 关联：PF₃ 的分子几何与极性。
- **1b-31** For molecules with only one central atom, how many lone pairs on the central atom guarantees molecular polarity?
  - 关联：孤对电子保证分子有极性。
- **1b-55** The ease with which the charge distribution in a molecule can be distorted by an external electrical field is called the ________.
  - 关联：极化率：电荷分布被外电场扭曲的难易。
- **1b-56** Hydrogen bonding is a special case of ________.
  - 关联：氢键是偶极–偶极作用的特殊情形。
- **1b-67** What is the predominant intermolecular force in HCN?
  - 关联：HCN 的主导分子间力（偶极）。
